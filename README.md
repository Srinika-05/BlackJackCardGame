What is it: This Python project is something I created during my coding classes. It is to replicate a BlackJack Card game, where the user draws their card from a deck and 
a house draws the cards after the player. The points are counted up, and the winner is decided based on the rules of a simple BlackJack game.

How to run it: This project was created on Trinket, but it could also be used in VS Code. 

How to play: After obtaining the code, all one has to do to play the game is first, run the code. The user will be prompted to place a bet money based 
on the amount of money they have in their current wallet. After that, a card is drawn and the player is asked whether they would like to stand or hit. 
If the player types in stand, they end their turn completely and get the amount of points from their current hand. If the player types in hit, 
then another card is drawn and counted with the total points until the player goes over 21 points, or type in stand. Once that's over, 
the House draws a card from the deck until they reach 17 total points. If they go over, the player wins. Otherwise, if the House's total points are above 
the player's total points, the House wins. If they are equal, then the game ends in a tie.
